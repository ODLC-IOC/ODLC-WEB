Spain Market Entry: set up your company in Spain without losing months

One English-speaking point of contact that manages the notary, the Companies Registry, banks and advisors, from structure to your first months of activity. It is the ODLC journey, adapted for companies and investors coming from abroad.

The problem

Incorporating a company in Spain looks simple on paper. In practice, powers of attorney, NIE numbers, the bank account and KYC stall many foreign investors for months.

What is included

  • Choice of structure (company or branch) with your tax advisor.
  • NIE numbers for shareholders and directors, and powers of attorney signed abroad.
  • Incorporation, registration, tax ID and statutory books, plus the bank account with KYC prepared.
  • First obligations: foreign investment filing, tax and corporate calendar.
  • Support during your first months of activity, in English.
